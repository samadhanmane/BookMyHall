================================================================================
PART 1: NON-TECHNICAL OVERVIEW (WHAT IS THIS WEBSITE & HOW IS IT USED?)
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS THIS WEBSITE?
------------------------
This website is an all-in-one digital campus management portal built for 
colleges and educational institutions. It replaces traditional paper forms, 
endless phone calls, and manual registers with a smart, easy-to-use web portal 
where students, teachers, department heads, and campus staff can manage 
day-to-day college operations in one place.


2. WHAT REAL-WORLD PROBLEMS DOES IT SOLVE?
------------------------------------------
* Prevent Double-Bookings: Ensures auditoriums, seminar halls, computer labs, 
  and campus equipment are never booked by two people at the same time.
* Fast Food & Refreshment Ordering: Streamlines official food orders for college 
  events, meetings, and guest visits without paper vouchers.
* Track Campus Repairs easily: Ends forgotten complaints about broken ACs, 
  projectors, benches, or electrical outlets by providing a transparent repair 
  tracking ticket system.
* AI Chatbot Assistant: Allows staff and students to reserve campus facilities 
  simply by typing messages in plain English to a smart AI chatbot.


3. MAIN FEATURES & HOW THEY WORK FOR EVERYDAY USERS:
-----------------------------------------------------

A) FACILITY & RESOURCE BOOKING
   - Browse all available college facilities (auditoriums, labs, conference rooms).
   - Select a date and time slot. When you select a slot, it is temporarily locked 
     for 5 minutes so no one else can steal it while you fill in details.
   - Smart AI Chatbot: You can open the chatbot and say "Book the Main Auditorium 
     for tomorrow at 2 PM for a Tech Seminar", and it will guide and complete the 
     booking for you.

B) CANTEEN & EVENT REQUISITIONS (FOOD ORDERS)
   - When a department hosts an official event, faculty can request food, tea, 
     or snacks directly through the website.
   - The request automatically flows through an approval process:
     Faculty Request -> HOD Approval -> Registrar Stamp -> Director Approval -> Canteen Kitchen.
   - Canteen staff receive the approved order, cook the food, assign delivery 
     staff, and mark it delivered.

C) WORKSHOP & MAINTENANCE SYSTEM (REPAIRS)
   - If a projector, computer, fan, or light bulb breaks down in a classroom, 
     faculty can submit a maintenance repair ticket.
   - The ticket tracks progress step-by-step: 
     Submitted -> Approved by Department Head -> Assigned to Technician -> In Progress -> Fixed & Completed.
   - If a technician needs a replacement part, they can pause the ticket with a 
     reason and resume once the part arrives.


4. WHO USES THIS WEBSITE? (ROLES & USES)
----------------------------------------
- Students & Faculty (Requesters): Book halls, order event refreshments, and 
  report broken equipment.
- Department Heads (HODs): Approve booking requests, food orders, and repair 
  tickets for their department.
- College Director & Registrar: Provide financial verification and final approval 
  for official college requests.
- Canteen Staff: View approved food orders, manage preparation, and deliver orders.
- Maintenance Technicians / Workers: Receive assigned repair tasks, update job 
  progress, and report when repairs are done.
- College Administrators (Admins): Manage college user accounts, settings, and 
  view overall campus statistics.


================================================================================
PART 2: TECHNICAL OVERVIEW (FOR DEVELOPERS & SYSTEM ADMINISTRATORS)
================================================================================

1. ARCHITECTURE & STRUCTURE
---------------------------
- Architecture: Monorepo with decoupled Frontend and Backend services.
- Multi-Tenancy: Supports multiple independent educational institutions/colleges 
  on a single platform instance.


2. FRONTEND TECH STACK
----------------------
- Framework: React.js with TypeScript (powered by Vite)
- UI & Styling: Tailwind CSS & Shadcn UI components
- Icons: Lucide React
- State & Auth Management: React Context API, Session-based storage (clears token 
  on tab/browser close for enhanced security)


3. BACKEND TECH STACK
---------------------
- Server & API: Node.js with Express.js
- Database: MongoDB with Mongoose ODM
- AI Engine: Google Gemini Pro API (powers conversational slot reservations)
- Email Queue: Nodemailer (asynchronous background notifications for approval steps)
- Security: JWT Tokens, Express Rate-Limiter, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)


4. CORE WORKFLOW & LOGIC ENGINES
--------------------------------
- Concurrency Locking: 5-minute atomic slot reservation lock to prevent race 
  conditions during simultaneous bookings.
- Sequential Approval Pipeline: Multi-stage workflow engine governing Canteen 
  and Workshop module state transitions.
- SuperAdmin Analytics: Platform-wide dashboard tracking organization onboardings, 
  active colleges, system admins, and AI Chatbot API usage metrics.
